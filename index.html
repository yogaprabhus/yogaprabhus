01 / ABOUT

Clarity in credit.
Efficiency in execution.

I am a commercial credit underwriting professional with more than four years of experience in the BFSI domain, supporting US commercial lending through financial statement analysis, financial spreading, cash flow assessment, and credit risk analysis.

At Accenture, my work includes reviewing borrower financial information and US tax returns, performing ratio and trend analysis, calculating Global DSCR, and supporting covenant and portfolio monitoring. I work with a range of borrower types, including for-profit businesses, non-profit organizations, and high-net-worth borrowers.

I also look for ways to improve recurring workflows. Two Excel automation macros I developed reduce underwriting analysis time by approximately 10-15 minutes per case. My focus is to pair careful analytical work with practical, repeatable process improvements.
